# Block Jam — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026  
**Developer:** Yumoji Games Studio  
**Contact:** support@yumojigames.com  
**App:** Block Jam (`com.yumojigames.blockjam`)

## What we collect
- Game progress stored on your device (level, coins, lives, boosters, settings).
- If you watch ads: advertising identifiers and device info via LevelPlay / AdMob mediation partners, used to show ads.
- If you buy in-app products: purchase receipts via Google Play Billing (handled by Google; we store only that ads were removed / coins were granted).

## What we do not collect
- No account, email, or name is required to play.
- No precise GPS location.
- No contact list or photos.

## Ads and consent
In regions that require it (for example EEA/UK), we show a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads. You can open privacy options again from Settings → Language menu → Privacy.

## Purchases
Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

## Children
The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what ad SDKs may process under applicable rules and consent.

## Your choices
- Turn off personalized ads via the in-game privacy options (where required).
- Uninstall the app to delete on-device save data.
- Manage ad preferences in your device settings.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

## Contact
Questions: support@yumojigames.com
