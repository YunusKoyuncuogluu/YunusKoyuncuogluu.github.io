# Block Jam — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 23 November 2026  
**Developer:** Yumoji Games Studio  
**Contact:** support@yumojigames.com  
**App:** Block Jam: Color Match Puzzle (`com.yumojigames.blockjam`)

## What we collect
- **Game progress** (level, coins, lives, boosters, settings) stored on your device.
- **Play Games / Game Center (optional sign-in):** if you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center / iCloud (iOS), the game uses your player profile to unlock achievements, post your highest level to the leaderboard, and back up your progress to your own Google or Apple account so you can restore it on a new device. We do not receive your email or real name from these services.
- **Anonymous analytics (GameAnalytics):** we collect anonymous gameplay events (for example levels started or completed, boosters used, screens opened, purchases and ad impressions), together with an anonymous device identifier, device model, operating system version, app version and approximate country. This helps us find levels that are too hard and fix problems.
- **Ads:** if ads are shown, our ad partners (LevelPlay / Unity Ads, Google AdMob and other mediation partners) process your advertising identifier and device information to show ads and measure them.
- **Purchases:** in-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. We only store that ads were removed or coins were granted.

## What we do not collect
- No account, email address or name is required to play.
- No precise GPS location.
- No contact list, photos or microphone data.

## Ads and consent
In regions that require it (for example EEA/UK), we show a consent form (Google UMP) before personalized ads. You can open the privacy options again in the game: Settings → Language menu → Privacy.

## Purchases
Payments are processed by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

## Children
The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what ad and analytics SDKs may process under applicable rules and consent.

## Your choices
- Turn off personalized ads through the in-game privacy options (where required) or your device's advertising settings (you can also reset or delete your advertising ID there).
- Sign out of Google Play Games or Game Center, or delete the saved game in your Google or Apple account, to remove cloud data.
- Uninstall the app to delete on-device save data.
- To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email support@yumojigames.com.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
